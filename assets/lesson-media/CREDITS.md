## The live multitrack (ADR 058)

`kick-`, `snare-`, `drums-`, `bass-`, `guitar-` and `mix-live-0N.flac` are the
same two moments of the same concert as the vocal phrases (2151.6 s and
2627.8 s of the multitrack, 2026-07-06), every mic: kick in+out, snare top
+ bottom (bottom polarity flipped), drums = kick, snare, floor tom, hi-hat,
overheads; the mix adds bass, guitar and pipe, panned. Bogdan Korablev,
CC BY 4.0.
