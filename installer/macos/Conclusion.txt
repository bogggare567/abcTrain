  VST3          /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  Audio Unit    /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  Applications  /Applications/abcTrain

  (If you chose "Install for me only", the same folders under your own
  Home folder instead.)

YOUR DAW WILL NOT SEE THE PLUGINS UNTIL IT RESCANS

  Most hosts rescan when they next start, so quitting and reopening your
  DAW is usually enough. Ableton Live, Cubase and Studio One also have a
  rescan button in their plugin preferences. Logic scans Audio Units on
  launch and will validate the new ones the first time.

  If a plugin does not appear, this is almost always why.

NO DAW? OPEN THE APPS

  ABC Ear Trainer, ABC Learner EQ, ABC Learner Comp and ABC Learner Verb
  are in /Applications/abcTrain and run on their own. Start with ABC Ear
  Trainer - headphones or monitors, not laptop speakers: they hide both
  ends of the spectrum, which is most of what the exercises are about.

  The disk image you installed from also carries "Open Plugins Folder",
  which opens the folders above in the Finder.
