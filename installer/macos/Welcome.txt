Welcome to the abcTrain installer.

This installs the ABC Ear Trainer / ABC Learner EQ / ABC Learner Comp / ABC Learner Verb
plugins. On the next screen you can choose which plugins to install and,
for each one, which plugin format(s): VST3 (for Ableton Live, Cubase,
Reaper, Studio One, and most other DAWs), Audio Unit/AU (for Logic Pro
and GarageBand), or the Standalone app (runs on its own, no DAW needed).

After choosing your components, you'll be able to pick whether to
install for every user of this Mac or just your own account.

This is a pre-release build for private testing - see the license
agreement on the next screen.
